# Träningsprogram – Kvinna, 45 år

**Profil:** Kvinna, 45 år, 63 kg, 172 cm  
**Mål:** Stark och funktionell kropp, ökad rörlighet, skadefri  
**Begränsning:** Axelskada (bicepssena + skulderbladsarea) – inga tunga vertikala tryck med fullt ROM  
**Upplägg:** 4-dagars överkropp/underkropp-split + valfri dag 5  
**Parallell träning:** Värmeyoga, ~1 löppass/vecka  

---

## Pass A – Lower Body (Strength)

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Squat (hip-width, barbell) | 4 × 6–8 | Djup efter rörlighet |
| Romanian deadlift | 3 × 8–10 | Kontrollerat tempo ner |
| Bulgarian split squat | 3 × 10/leg | Fram fot på platt |
| Leg extension | 3 × 12–15 | |
| Lying leg curl | 3 × 10–12 | |
| Standing calf raise | 3 × 15–20 | |

---

## Pass B – Upper Body (Shoulder-adapted)

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Seated cable row (neutral grip) | 4 × 10–12 | Skulderbladen styr rörelsens slut |
| Incline dumbbell press (neutral grip, ~30°) | 3 × 8–10 | Undviker impingement – justera vinkel efter axeln |
| Lat pulldown (neutral/supinated grip) | 3 × 10–12 | Dra till övre bröst, inte bakom nacke |
| Face pull (rope/cable, high attachment) | 3 × 15 | Skulderbladsretraktion, liten extern rotation |
| Dumbbell bicep curl | 3 × 12 | |
| Dumbbell shoulder press (if pain-free) | 2 × 10 | Testa smärt-ROM, hoppa om det gör ont |

## Day 5 (Optional)

Active recovery, extra yoga, light walk – or repeat whichever pass felt weakest that week.

---

## Progression

Increase weight when all sets feel controlled and easy two sessions in a row. Prioritize technique and pain-free movement in the shoulder before adding load.
